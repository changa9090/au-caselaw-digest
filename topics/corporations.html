Wardingarri Aboriginal Corporation and Registrar of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Corporations (Guidance and Appeals Panel)

8 Oct 2026Administrative Review Tribunal[2026] ARTA 2281Tribunal reviewCorporations
Wardingarri Aboriginal Corporation, a registered native title body corporate under the CATSI Act, sought review and a stay of the Registrar’s 22 July 2026 decision concerning its membership eligibility rules and director terms. The Tribunal granted a conditional stay of the membership eligibility amendment but refused to stay the director term amendment. Decision: Wardingarri Aboriginal Corporation and Registrar of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Corporations (Guidance and Appeals Panel) [2026] ARTA 2281 (8 October 2026).
Key holding

Under s 32(2) of the Tribunal Act, the Tribunal may stay the operation or implementation of a reviewable decision if satisfied it is desirable to ensure the effectiveness of the review.

However, on application by a party to a proceeding for review of a reviewable decision, the Tribunal may make an order staying or otherwise affecting the operation or implementation of the decision if the Tribunal considers that it is desirable to do so for the purpose of ensuring the effectiveness of the review.
