Martin v Office of the Director of Public Prosecutions (NSW)

20 Oct 2026Supreme Court of New South Wales[2026] NSWSC 1288Tribunal reviewVisa nominationCriminal law
Before: Hamill J Decision: (1) The appeal is allowed.(2) The decision of Acting Magistra
This was a NSW Supreme Court appeal against Local Court convictions for a sexual act towards a child and attempted voyeurism. Hamill J allowed the appeal because the Acting Magistrate’s ex tempore reasons were inadequate and failed to address the onus and standard of proof and key exculpatory evidence and submissions. The DPP conceded error.
Key holding

A conviction will be vitiated by an error of law where the judicial officer’s reasons fail to disclose the onus and standard of proof applied and fail to explain why exculpatory evidence and submissions were rejected.

The failure to provide adequate reasons - including but not limited to, the failure to mention the requirement of proof beyond a reasonable doubt - amounted to a fundamental failure of process and amounted to a clear error of law.

Coe and Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority

13 Oct 2026Administrative Review Tribunal[2026] ARTA 2317Tribunal reviewVisa refusal reviewCriminal law
Jason Coe applied to have Jardini Pty Ltd’s Great Barrier Reef Marine Park permit transferred to him personally. The Authority refused, and on review the Tribunal found he was a suitable person but still affirmed refusal because other matters under s 120(c) of the GBRMP Regulations concerned the objects of the GBRMP Act and orderly management of the Marine Park. The Tribunal also resolved procedural issues about production, redactions and deciding on the papers.
Key holding

Even where the Tribunal does not find a proposed transferee unsuitable, it may refuse transfer under s 120(c) because of other matters relevant to the objects of the GBRMP Act or management of the Marine Park.

For the reasons set out in more detail below, the Tribunal has not found the Applicant to be an unsuitable person. However it does believe that at this time, and having regard to the present circumstances, there are matters which are relevant to the proposed transfer and the objects of the GBRMP Act and management of the Marine Park such that the transfer of the Permit should not be approved.
