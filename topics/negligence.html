The appellant challenged damages for admitted medical negligence after receiving hormone replacement tablets instead of contraception, leading to pregnancy, termination and psychiatric injury. The Court of Appeal dismissed the appeal, upholding the $39,452 trial verdict and the trial judge's findings on exaggeration, credibility and competing causes of psychiatric symptoms.
