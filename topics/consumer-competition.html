JACOBS -v- HER HONOUR MAGISTRATE WARD

9 Oct 2026Supreme Court of Western Australia[2026] WASC 435Migration: court challengeConsumer & competition
Before: WHITBY J HEARD : 20 & 31 AUGUST 2026 DELIVERED : 9 OCTOBER 2026 FILE NO/S : CIV
Ms Jacobs, a public housing tenant, sought judicial review of a Magistrates Court decision dismissing her Residential Tenancies Act claims and finding no jurisdiction over her Australian Consumer Law claims. The Supreme Court found a reasonably arguable case and identified jurisdictional errors arising from denial of natural justice and misconstruction of RTA provisions. The decision was quashed in part and remitted to the Magistrates Court, differently constituted.
Key holding

On judicial review under s 36 MCA and s 26(2) RTA, an inferior court’s decision is affected by jurisdictional error only where it fails to exercise judicial power itself, including by misconceiving its statutory function or power, rather than merely making an error within jurisdiction.

These cases confirm that the relevant inquiry upon an application for judicial review of a decision of an inferior court is whether there has been a failure of the judicial officer to exercise judicial power itself or whether the judicial officer has merely made an error while exercising that judicial power. The former will generally constitute jurisdictional error; the latter will not.
