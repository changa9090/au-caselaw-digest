28 Sep 2025

Vixon Kumar v Star Track Express Pty Limited

28 Sep 2025Fair Work Commission[2025] FWC 2719Employment & workplace
Mr Vixon Kumar was summarily dismissed by Star Track Express for serious misconduct after throwing freight (including after being told to stop), unsafely operating an electric pallet jack carrying a 1.2 tonne load, and taking unauthorised videos/photos of colleagues. The Commission found the misconduct proven, partly on video evidence, and rejected the applicant's claims of targeting, differential treatment and union-related discrimination, finding no reverse onus applies in unfair dismissal proceedings.

Application by Ms Lihui Wang

26 Sep 2025Fair Work Commission[2025] FWC 2885Employment & workplace
Ms Wang, a former fixed-term lecturer at TAFE SA, applied for a stop bullying order against her employer and supervisor, alleging bullying including a performance improvement plan and non-renewal of her contract. Construction, Forestry and Maritime Employees Union (105N) v DP World Brisbane Pty Ltd

23 Sep 2025Fair Work Commission[2025] FWC 2150Employment & workplace
The CFMEU (MUA) brought a s739 dispute against DP World Brisbane about how personal leave accruals of 13 days per year should be calculated for Variable Salary Employees (VSEs) and Fixed Salary Employees (FSEs) under the DP World Brisbane Enterprise Agreement 2024. The Commission held that the notional day construction applies, that VSE personal leave accrues at one-twentieth of rostered hours per year with hours over 35 per week treated as overtime, and that FSEs accrue 91 hours (3.5 hours per fortnight) based on 35 ordinary hours per week.

Legal words, explained

Medium neutral citation (e.g. [2026] NSWSC 1234)
The standard reference number Australian courts give each decision: year, court code, and sequence number. It identifies the decision even before it appears in a law report.
Court / tribunal
Courts decide disputes between parties and can hear appeals. Tribunals (like the ART) re-examine government decisions on their merits, in a quicker, less formal process.
Judicial review
A court checking whether a government or tribunal decision was legally made. The court looks at legal errors, not whether it agrees with the outcome.
Merits review
A tribunal (usually the Administrative Review Tribunal) re-making a government decision from scratch, considering all the facts again.
Visa cancellation (s 501)
Under section 501 of the Migration Act 1958, a visa can be refused or cancelled on 'character' grounds, often because of a criminal record. Many tribunal cases on this site are appeals against such cancellations.
Set aside / affirmed
When a tribunal or appeal court 'sets aside' a decision, it is cancelled and usually replaced. 'Affirmed' means the original decision stands.
Applicant / respondent
The applicant is the person who brought the case; the respondent is the party responding to it (in tribunal matters, often a government minister or agency).
