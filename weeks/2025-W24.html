EDI Rail PPP Maintenance Pty Ltd v "Automotive, Food, Metals, Engineering, Printing and Kindred Industries Union" known as the Australian Manufacturing Workers' Union (AMWU)-New South Wales Branch (188V-NSW)

13 Jun 2025Fair Work Commission[2025] FWC 1649Employment & workplaceContracts
EDI Rail sought orders under s.418 to stop alleged unprotected industrial action after eight employees refused to perform night-shift duties on 10-12 June 2025 in protest at a new roster. The Commission found the employees' safety concerns about the new roster were not a reasonable concern about an imminent risk to health or safety, so their action was unprotected industrial action. Orders were made against the eight employees and Mr Peach as organiser, but not against the AMWU, which had recommended members not cease work.

Ms Wendy Bazley v Kare Qld Pty Ltd

13 Jun 2025Fair Work Commission[2025] FWC 1647Employment & workplace
Ms Bazley lodged a general protections application alleging adverse action after her employment with Kare Qld ended. The employer raised a jurisdictional objection that she was not dismissed. The Commission found that Ms Bazley had unilaterally repudiated a material aspect of her employment contract (refusing team leader duties and reducing hours), which Kare accepted, and that the employment relationship ended as a consequence of her own actions rather than the employer's initiative. The jurisdictional objection was upheld.

Legal words, explained

Medium neutral citation (e.g. [2026] NSWSC 1234)
The standard reference number Australian courts give each decision: year, court code, and sequence number. It identifies the decision even before it appears in a law report.
Court / tribunal
Courts decide disputes between parties and can hear appeals. Tribunals (like the ART) re-examine government decisions on their merits, in a quicker, less formal process.
Judicial review
A court checking whether a government or tribunal decision was legally made. The court looks at legal errors, not whether it agrees with the outcome.
Merits review
A tribunal (usually the Administrative Review Tribunal) re-making a government decision from scratch, considering all the facts again.
Visa cancellation (s 501)
Under section 501 of the Migration Act 1958, a visa can be refused or cancelled on 'character' grounds, often because of a criminal record. Many tribunal cases on this site are appeals against such cancellations.
Set aside / affirmed
When a tribunal or appeal court 'sets aside' a decision, it is cancelled and usually replaced. 'Affirmed' means the original decision stands.
Applicant / respondent
The applicant is the person who brought the case; the respondent is the party responding to it (in tribunal matters, often a government minister or agency).
