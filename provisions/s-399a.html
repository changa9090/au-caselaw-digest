Robert Palmer v Keramos Pty Ltd

21 Oct 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 3945Employment & workplace
In [2026] FWC 3945, Robert Palmer lodged an unfair dismissal application under s 394 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) (FW Act) out of time, and Keramos Pty Ltd objected on jurisdiction. The Fair Work Commission dismissed the application under s 399A after Mr Palmer repeatedly failed to comply with directions and to respond to the employer’s dismissal application. The merits and extension-of-time question were not determined.
Key holding

Under s 399A of the FW Act, the FWC may dismiss an application where an applicant unreasonably fails to comply with FWC directions, on the employer’s application and in the exercise of the FWC’s discretion.

I am satisfied that Mr Palmer has unreasonably failed to comply with directions of the FWC relating to this Application. On the application of Keramos and in the exercise of my discretion under section 399A of the FW Act, I have decided to dismiss the Application.

Manpreetsingh Nanda v NTI Limited

12 Oct 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 3659Employment & workplace
Mr Nanda lodged an unfair dismissal application under s.394 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) against NTI Limited. He repeatedly failed to attend Commission conciliations and a case management conference and did not respond to directions on the employer’s s.399A dismissal application. The Commission dismissed his unfair dismissal application under s.399A.
Key holding

The power to dismiss a substantive application should only be exercised cautiously and sparingly because dismissal extinguishes the applicant’s right to have the application heard and determined according to law.

The power to dismiss a substantive application should only be exercised cautiously and sparingly because it results in the complete extinguishment of an applicant's right to have their application heard and determined according to law before they have had their "day in court.
