May 2021 (1)

FED20 v Minister for Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs

18 May 2021FCCA[2021] FCCA 1063Migration: court challengeMigration
The applicant, an Afghan citizen in immigration detention, sought an extension of time under s 477 to challenge a Tribunal decision that it lacked jurisdiction to review his protection visa refusal because his review application was filed late. The Court found the delay explanation satisfactory but no arguable case on the merits, since the Tribunal correctly held it had no power to extend time. The extension of time was dismissed, though the Court recommended ministerial intervention given the Taliban takeover of Afghanistan.
