2432312 (Migration)

23 Sep 2024Administrative Review Tribunal[2024] AATA 3685Tribunal reviewVisa cancellationVisa refusal reviewMigration
Before: Melissa McAdam
The applicant, a Pakistani citizen, applied for a Bridging E (Class WE) visa after being released on parole and detained under s 189. The delegate refused the Subclass 050 visa because it was not satisfied he would comply with condition 8564 (not engage in criminal conduct). The Tribunal affirmed the refusal, finding acceptable departure arrangements but insufficient assurance he would abide by visa conditions.
Key holding

Under cl 050.223, the Tribunal must be satisfied at the time of decision that, if a Subclass 050 bridging visa is granted, the applicant will abide by the conditions imposed.

Clause 050.223 requires that the Tribunal is satisfied at the time of decision, that if a bridging visa is granted to the applicant, the applicant will abide by any conditions imposed on it.

2429896 (Migration)

3 Sep 2024Administrative Review Tribunal[2024] AATA 3497Tribunal reviewVisa cancellationVisa refusal reviewMigration
Before: Antonio Dronjic
The applicant, a Nepalese citizen in immigration detention, applied for a Bridging E (Class WE) visa based on a pending protection visa application. The Tribunal found the visa ground was met but refused the visa because it was not satisfied he would abide by conditions, including no work, reporting, address notification and no criminal conduct, regardless of any security. The Tribunal affirmed the delegate’s refusal.
Key holding

For a Subclass 050 Bridging (General) visa, cl 050.223 requires the Tribunal to be satisfied the applicant will abide by conditions; if not satisfied regardless of any security, the criterion is not met.

If the Tribunal is satisfied that the applicant will abide by the conditions if security of a particular amount is required, the applicant meets cl 050.223. However, if not satisfied that the applicant will comply with the conditions, regardless of any security that may be imposed, cl 050.223 is not met.
