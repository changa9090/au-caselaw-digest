Mr Liam Torr v A & W Produce Pty Ltd

13 Oct 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 3924Employment & workplace
Mr Liam Torr lodged a general protections dismissal application under s 365 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) two days after the 21-day statutory deadline. The Fair Work Commission granted an extension of time after accepting his homelessness was a substantial reason for the delay and finding exceptional circumstances. The matter will proceed to conciliation.
Key holding

Before time can be extended under s 366(2), the Commission must be satisfied that exceptional circumstances exist, which is a high threshold.

The Commission must first be satisfied that there are "exceptional circumstances" before a discretion to extend time is enlivened. This is a high hurdle.

Ms Alex Culhane v Psychsense Therapeutic Services Pty Ltd & Chantelle Chapman

12 Oct 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 3893Employment & workplace
Ms Culhane applied under s.365 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth), alleging dismissal by Psychsense Therapeutic Service Pty Ltd and Ms Chapman. The Commission had to determine a jurisdictional objection that she had not been dismissed because she resigned. Deputy President Clancy found there was no dismissal and dismissed the application.
Key holding

For a s.365 application at the material time, the Commission lacks jurisdiction unless the applicant was actually dismissed under s.386(1), meaning termination on the employer’s initiative or a resignation forced by employer conduct.

I have concluded that in this case there was no dismissal within the meaning of s.386 of the Act because I am satisfied there was no termination of the Applicant's employment on the Respondent's initiative and nor have I been persuaded that the Applicant was forced to resign because of conduct or a course of conduct engaged in by the Respondent .

Mrs Narelle Wild v Hireup Pty Ltd [2026] FWC 1584 (1 May 2026)

1 May 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 1584Employment & workplace
This is a Fair Work Commission general protections dismissal application under s.365 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth). Mrs Wild lodged her application 4 days after the 21-day limit and sought an extension under s.366(2). The Commission found no exceptional circumstances and dismissed the application.
Key holding

The Commission may extend time for a s.365 general protections dismissal application only if satisfied that exceptional circumstances exist under s.366(2).

Under s.366 of the FW Act, the Commission has the discretion grant an extension of time for the making of a general protections application involving dismissal. The Commission's discretion is only enlivened if it is satisfied that there are exceptional circumstances.
