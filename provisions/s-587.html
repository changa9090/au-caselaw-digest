Mr Amos Harradine v Eastern Maar Aboriginal Corporation Rntbc [2026] FWC 1325 (16 April 2026)

16 Apr 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 1325Employment & workplace
Mr Harradine lodged an unfair dismissal application under s.394 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) against Eastern Maar Aboriginal Corporation Rntbc. The employer raised jurisdictional objections, including that the application was out of time and barred by an executed deed of release dated 29 September 2025. The Commission dismissed the application under s.587 because the applicant repeatedly failed to prosecute it, comply with directions and attend hearings.
Key holding

The Commission’s power to dismiss under s.587 extends beyond the enumerated grounds to cases of failure to prosecute, including non-compliance with directions and unexplained failure to provide a proper explanation.

I am satisfied the power to dismiss can extend to circumstances where an applicant has failed to prosecute his or her case, including by non-compliance with directions or orders and a repeated failure to provide a proper explanation for that non-compliance.

Ms Ashleigh May Llewellyn v Sexyland Australia Pty Ltd [2026] FWC 1038 (16 April 2026)

16 Apr 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 1038Employment & workplace
Ms Llewellyn made an unfair dismissal application under s.394 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) after employment from 26 June to 27 August 2025. Because her employment was less than the minimum employment period, the Fair Work Commission dismissed the application on its own initiative under s.587. The Commission found no reasonable prospects of success.
Key holding

An unfair dismissal application cannot succeed unless the applicant has completed the minimum employment period under ss.382 and 383, and a clearly ineligible claim may be dismissed under s.587 for having no reasonable prospects.

Sections 382 and 383 of the Act provide that a person is only eligible to make an unfair dismissal application if they have completed a minimum period of employment before their dismissal.

Samantha Sebbens v Invocare Australia Pty Limited [2026] FWC 1314 (16 April 2026)

16 Apr 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 1314Employment & workplace
This is an unfair dismissal application under s.394 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth). The Applicant failed to comply with Fair Work Commission directions and did not respond to Commission correspondence. The Commission dismissed the application for want of prosecution under s.587.
Key holding

The Commission may dismiss an application for want of prosecution under s.587 where the applicant repeatedly fails to comply with directions and does not respond to Commission correspondence, showing no intention to pursue the matter.

Having regard to the Applicant's repeated non-compliance with the Directions of the Commission and her failure to respond to correspondence, I am satisfied that the Applicant has not demonstrated an intention to pursue her application. Accordingly, I have determined that the application should be dismissed for want of prosecution pursuant to s.587 of the Act.

Max Papageorge v 62 Monash Road Pty Ltd [2026] FWC 1259 (13 April 2026)

13 Apr 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 1259Employment & workplace
Max Papageorge made an unfair dismissal application under s 394 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) after about four months’ employment with 62 Monash Road Pty Ltd. The Fair Work Commission dismissed the application on its own initiative because the applicant had not completed the minimum employment period and the application had no reasonable prospects of success.
Key holding

The Commission may dismiss an unfair dismissal application on its own initiative for no reasonable prospects only if it is very clear there is no reasonable prospect of success and there are no disputed facts that could affect the outcome.

An application should not be dismissed under s 587 unless it is very clear that there are no reasonable prospects of success. As such the power under s 587 is not available if there are disputed facts that could affect the outcome of the proceedings.
