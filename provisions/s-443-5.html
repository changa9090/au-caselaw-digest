Australian Municipal, Administrative, Clerical and Services Union v Queensland Rail Transit Authority T/A Queensland Rail [2026] FWC 892 (18 March 2026)

18 Mar 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 892Employment & workplace
The ASU applied under s.437 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) for a protected action ballot order for Queensland Rail employees covered by the Queensland Rail Train Control Enterprise Agreement 2023. The employer did not object if the notice period for industrial action was amended, and the ASU consented to an amended notice period on the basis of relevant exceptional circumstances. Deputy President Hampton determined the matter on the papers, was satisfied the statutory requirements were met, and issued an order.
Key holding

Where relevant exceptional circumstances exist, the Commission may extend the written notice period for protected industrial action under s.443(5), subject to the statutory minimum in s.414(2)(a).

I am satisfied that there are relevant exceptional circumstances required by s.443(5), justifying an extended period of written notice for the purposes of s.414(2)(b) of the Act when taking industrial action set out in the Order made by the Commission.

The CFMEU applied under s.437 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) for a protected action ballot order covering certain Queensland Rail employees. Queensland Rail did not object if the notice period for industrial action was amended, and the CFMEU accepted relevant exceptional circumstances and consented to the amended notice period. The Commission determined the matter on the papers and made the order as amended, with TrueVote as the electronic ballot agent.

The minimum extended notice period of five (5) calendar days applies to the items listed within Clause 5 of the Order.
