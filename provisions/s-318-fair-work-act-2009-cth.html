Application by Bendigo Health Foundation Ltd

13 Oct 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 3927Employment & workplace
Bendigo Health Foundation Ltd applied under s.318 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) for orders that a transferable public-sector enterprise agreement not cover it and three transferring employees following a transfer of business from Bendigo Health. The employees consented, their service and accrued entitlements were preserved, and the Commission found the s.318(3) factors favoured disapplying the Agreement.
Key holding

The Commission may make an order under s.318 only where it is satisfied that a transfer of business under s.311 has occurred or is likely to occur.

The power to make orders under s.318 of the Act is premised on the Commission being satisfied that there has been, or that there is likely to be a transfer of business for the purposes of s.311 of the Act.

Dpg Services Pty Ltd Trading AS Opal Healthcare [2026] FWC 985 (2 April 2026)

2 Apr 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 985Employment & workplace
DPG Services Pty Ltd t/a Opal HealthCare applied under s.318 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) for orders displacing the Sunnymeade Support Staff Enterprise Agreement 2024 and applying the Opal HealthCare (Queensland) Enterprise Agreement 2023 to 49 transferring aged-care employees after acquiring the Sunnymeade Park facility. The Commission granted the orders after accepting the employer’s preservation undertakings and finding the employees would not be disadvantaged overall.
Key holding

Under s.318, the Commission may grant an order transferring coverage to the new employer’s enterprise agreement where undertakings and the new agreement’s overall benefits mean transferring employees will not be disadvantaged on terms and conditions.

I am satisfied the Applicant's undertakings, coupled with the more beneficial terms and conditions of employment under the Opal Agreement, enable the Commission to be satisfied that, on an overall basis the transferring employees will not be disadvantaged by the proposed orders in relation to their terms and conditions of employment and that the consideration in s.318(3)(b) weighs in favour of granting the Application.

The Commission granted orders limited to Mr Kerr and Mr Quadizar but refused the broader claim for future transferring employees.
Key holding

Although s 318 permits orders covering future transferring employees, the Commission should not make orders of such wide import where the evidence is confined to the circumstances of the named employees.

OZ Minerals Carrapateena Pty Ltd [2026] FWC 669 (27 February 2026)

27 Feb 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 669Employment & workplace
OZ Minerals sought orders under s.318 of the Fair Work Act 2009 to prevent a labour-hire enterprise agreement covering Mr McLeod from applying after he accepted direct employment. The Commission found there was no transfer of business under s.311 because none of the required connections between the old and new employer existed. It therefore declined to make the orders.
Key holding

A s.318 order has no utility unless there is first a transfer of business under s.311, including a connection under ss.311(3)-(6), because otherwise there is no transferable instrument.

However, I find that there is not a connection between Red Appointments and OZ Minerals of the kind described in ss. 311(3)-(6) and therefore, no transfer of business has occurred. Since there has not been a transfer of business, there is no transferrable instrument and accordingly there is no utility in making the orders sought and I decline to do so.
