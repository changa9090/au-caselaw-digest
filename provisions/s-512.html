February 2026 (1)

Construction, Forestry and Maritime Employees Union-Construction and General Division, New South Wales Divisional Branch [2026] FWC 664 (27 February 2026)

27 Feb 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 664Employment & workplace
The CFMEU applied under s 512 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) for a right-of-entry permit for Mr Hamasolo Keith Jaleel Alone, a NSW State Organiser. The Commission considered his training, largely clean permit-qualification record, a 2024 pub assault dealt with by good behaviour bond without conviction, withdrawn 2025 charges, and earlier non-disclosure. Deputy President Saunders was satisfied on balance that Mr Alone is fit and proper and issued the permit.
Key holding

Under s 512, the Commission may issue an entry permit if, after weighing all s 513(1) permit qualification matters, it is satisfied on balance that the official is fit and proper to hold the permit.

After taking into account and weighing each of the permit qualification matters set out in s 513(1)(a) to (g) of the Act, I am satisfied, on balance, that Mr Alone is a fit and proper person to hold a right of entry permit. The matters which weigh in favour of a finding that Mr Alone is a fit and proper person to hold a right of entry permit outweigh those that weigh against such a conclusion.
