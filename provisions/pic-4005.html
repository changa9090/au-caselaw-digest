July 2026 (1)

Lyana (Migration)

17 Jul 2026Administrative Review Tribunal[2026] ARTA 2381Tribunal reviewVisa nominationVisa refusal reviewMigration
This is an ARTA review of a delegate’s refusal of a Subclass 500 student visa to Ms Lyana as a secondary applicant. The delegate found no evidence of medical assessment, but departmental records showed a finalised health clearance, so the Tribunal set aside the refusal and remitted with an order that PIC 4005(1)(aa) is met.
Key holding

Where the departmental records confirm that the required medical assessment has been undertaken and finalised, the applicant satisfies PIC 4005(1)(aa) for cl 500.317.

On the evidence before it, the Tribunal finds that Ms Lyana has undertaken the required medical assessment and, as a consequence, she meets the requirement in PIC 4005(1)(aa) of Schedule 4 to the Regulations for the purposes of cl 500.317 of Schedule 2 to the Regulations.
