Dorothy Lazenkas v Mondelez Australia Pty Ltd

16 Oct 2026Fair Work Commission (Full Bench)[2026] FWCFB 280Employment & workplace
Ms Lazenkas made an unfair dismissal application, then later filed a general protections application in which she stated she withdrew the earlier unfair dismissal claim. The Full Bench considered whether s 725 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) barred the general protections application because the earlier application had not been withdrawn before the second application was made. By majority, the appeal was dismissed, with Vice President Gibian dissenting.
Key holding

Under the Fair Work Act, if an unfair dismissal application has been made, it must have been withdrawn before a subsequent s 365 general protections application is made; simultaneous withdrawal in the later application does not disengage s 725.

We do not consider that the language and structure of ss 725 and 729(1)(b)(i) can accommodate the simultaneous making of a second prescribed application and a withdrawal of a first. The withdrawal must precede the second application.

Mr Mark Shorrock v Cinc Switchboards Pty Ltd

12 Oct 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 3911Employment & workplace
Mark Shorrock made a s.365 general protections application against Cinc Switchboards Pty Ltd, alleging constructive dismissal. The Commission determined a jurisdictional objection that he had resigned voluntarily and was not dismissed. It upheld the objection and dismissed the application.
Key holding

A resignation is a dismissal under s.386(1)(b) only where the employer’s conduct or course of conduct was intended to bring about the resignation or had the probable result that the employee had no effective or real choice but to resign, with employer conduct being essential.

To satisfy the test for constructive dismissal requires a substantive and demonstratable course of conduct aimed at bringing about the end of the employment. This action is evident in the actions of the Employer, and the intended result of the conduct to bring about the Applicant's resignation; or that that would be the probable result.
