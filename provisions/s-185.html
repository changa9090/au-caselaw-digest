Seventh-Day Adventist Schools (South New South Wales) Limited

8 Oct 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWCA 2771Employment & workplace
The Fair Work Commission approved the Seventh-day Adventist Schools (South New South Wales) Ltd Enterprise Agreement 2026–2029, a single-enterprise agreement covering educational services. The employer provided s.190 undertakings, and the Commission was satisfied the relevant approval requirements were met. Because the Agreement lacked compliant flexibility, consultation and delegates’ rights terms, model or award terms were taken to be included.
Key holding

The Commission may approve a single-enterprise agreement under s.185 where, subject to accepted s.190 undertakings, it is satisfied the relevant approval requirements in ss.186, 187, 188 and 190 are met.

Subject to the undertakings referred to above, I am satisfied that each of the requirements of ss.186, 187, 188 and 190 of the FW Act as are relevant to this application for approval have been met.

June 2026 (1)

Application by St Vincent’s Private Hospitals Ltd Trading AS St Vincent’s Private Hospital Melbourne

25 Jun 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 2348Employment & workplace
St Vincent's Private Hospitals applied under s 185 for approval of a single-enterprise agreement. The ASU opposed approval, and the Commission found that a significant number of casual employees who were 'employed at the time' (ongoing casuals) were not given an opportunity to vote because the employer only included casuals who worked during a narrow access period. Because those eligible casuals were excluded from the vote, the Commission was not satisfied the agreement was genuinely agreed to, and the application was dismissed.

October 2025 (1)

Application by Aldi Foods Pty Limited As General Partner Of Aldi Stores (A Limited Partnership) Trading AS Aldi Stores

20 Oct 2025Fair Work Commission[2025] FWC 3130Employment & workplace
ALDI applied under s 185 for approval of three single-enterprise agreements. The Commission found the agreements did not pass the better off overall test, primarily because hourly-rate part-time employees faced unpredictable working hours, a key benefit under the Storage Services and Wholesale Award 2020. ALDI declined to provide an undertaking, so the Commission proposed to exercise its power under s 191A to amend the agreements to require agreed regular patterns of work, and listed the matter for directions to seek the parties' views.

June 2025 (1)

Application by I-MED Regional Pty Ltd

2 Jun 2025Fair Work Commission[2025] FWC 1477Employment & workplace
I-MED applied under s 185 for approval of the I-MED Regional Victoria Enterprise Agreement 2023. The Commission found that the employer did not take all reasonable steps to explain the terms and effect of the updated classification structure, and that this was a procedural error that was not minor and could not be disregarded under s 188(5)(aa). As the Commission could not be satisfied the agreement was genuinely agreed to, the application for approval was dismissed.
