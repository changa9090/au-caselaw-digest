Tiwari v Minister for Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs

5 May 2021FCCA[2021] FCCA 940Migration: court challengeMigration
The applicant sought judicial review of a Tribunal decision affirming the refusal of a Student (subclass 500) visa because he was not enrolled in a registered course of study at the time of decision, as required by cl 500.211. The Court found none of the applicant's grounds raised an arguable case for jurisdictional error, noting the enrolment requirement was a mandatory primary criterion with no discretion to waive it. The application was dismissed under r 44.12 of the Federal Circuit Court Rules.

Kanyan v Minister for Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs

5 May 2021FCCA[2021] FCCA 931Migration: court challengeMigration
The applicant sought judicial review of a Tribunal decision affirming the refusal of a Student visa because he was not enrolled in a registered course. The Court found no arguable jurisdictional error: the Tribunal was entitled to decide the matter on the papers given the applicant's consent, had no duty to invite him to a hearing or disclose his own information under s 359A, and had put him on notice that the enrolment criterion was in issue. The amended application was dismissed under r 44.12 of the Federal Circuit Court Rules.
