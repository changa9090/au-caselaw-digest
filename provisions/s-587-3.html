Kerry-Anne Lowe v Vision And Goals Pty Ltd [2026] FWC 1186 (9 April 2026)

9 Apr 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 1186Employment & workplace
Kerry-Anne Lowe applied under s 394 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) for an unfair dismissal remedy against Vision And Goals Pty Ltd. The Fair Work Commission dismissed the application on its own initiative because the applicant appeared to have served only about 3 months, less than the minimum employment period, and had not paid or obtained waiver of the filing fee. The applicant did not respond to Commission correspondence.
Key holding

Under s 587, the FWC may dismiss an unfair dismissal application on its own initiative where it has no reasonable prospects of success because the applicant has not met the minimum employment period and the application was not made in accordance with the Act due to non-payment or non-waiver of the fee.

I am satisfied that the Application has no reasonable prospect of success within the meaning of s 587(1)(c) and that it was not made in accordance with s 587(1)(a) of the Act. It is therefore appropriate in the circumstances to dismiss the Application on the Commission's own initiative using the facility available in s 587(3)(a).
