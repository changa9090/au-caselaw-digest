Application by UGL Engineering Pty Ltd [2026] FWC 1141 (1 April 2026)

1 Apr 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 1141Employment & workplace
UGL Engineering Pty Ltd applied under s 319 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) for orders extending two UGL/CEPU enterprise agreements to non-transferring employees performing transferring work after an internal UGL group realignment. The relevant unions did not oppose, and the Commission granted the applications after considering the s 319(3) factors, including that one agreement had passed its nominal expiry date. The decision is a straightforward transfer-of-business coverage order.
Key holding

An enterprise agreement that has passed its nominal expiry date is not automatically a bar to a s 319(1)(b) coverage order; it is weighed with the circumstances and other s 319(3) factors.

While the UGL and CEPU Electrical Division Queensland Regional Enterprise Agreement 2020-2024 has passed its nominal expiry date of 31 January 2025, in circumstances where UGL is seeking to appropriately align their operations, and that agreement is the only subject agreement to have passed its expiry date, I do not consider that expiry to be a bar to the granting of the orders sought.
