Hayley Bannon v Experience Co Admin Pty Ltd [2026] FWC 1258 (13 April 2026)

13 Apr 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 1258Employment & workplace
The Fair Work Commission dismissed Hayley Bannon’s s 394 unfair dismissal application on its own initiative under s 587(1)(c) of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth). The Commission found the application had no reasonable prospects because the Applicant’s own information indicated she had not completed the minimum employment period. No merits hearing was required.
Key holding

The Commission may dismiss an unfair dismissal application on its own initiative under s 587(1)(c) and s 587(3)(a) where it is satisfied the application has no reasonable prospect of success.

I am satisfied that the Application has no reasonable prospect of success within the meaning of s 587(1)(c) of the Act. It is therefore appropriate in the circumstances to dismiss the Application on the Commission's own initiative using the facility available in s 587(3)(a).

The Fair Work Commission dismissed Mr Turbill’s unfair dismissal application on its own initiative under s 587(1)(c) of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth). The applicant commenced employment on 1 August 2025 and was dismissed effective 23 January 2026, meaning he had not completed the minimum employment period on the dates given. He failed to respond to Commission requests for information about the minimum employment period.
Key holding

Subject to procedural fairness, the Commission may dismiss an unfair dismissal application on its own initiative under s 587 where there is no reasonable prospect of success and no live facts in issue.

Subject to affording procedural fairness, s 587 of the Act allows the Commission to dismiss an application on the Commission's own initiative in the early stages of proceedings. Protracted proceedings can be avoided when there is no reasonable prospect of an outcome other than the dismissal of the application.
