May 2024 (1)

Zhu v Minister for Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs

3 May 2024Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia[2024] FedCFamC2G 411Migration: court challengeMigration
The applicant sought judicial review of an Administrative Appeals Tribunal decision affirming a refusal of a visa based on failure to satisfy PIC 4003(b), which relates to weapons of mass destruction proliferation. The Court found no jurisdictional error on the part of the Tribunal and held that PIC 4003(b) and clause 866.222A are valid and not repugnant to s 501 of the Migration Act. The amended application for review was dismissed.

February 2023 (1)

Chen (Migration)

11 Feb 2023Administrative Review Tribunal[2023] AATA 297Tribunal reviewVisa refusal reviewMigration
Before: David Barker
The applicant, a PhD student in photonics, sought review of a refusal of a Student (Temporary) (Class TU) subclass 500 visa. The refusal was based on the Foreign Minister's determination under PIC 4003(b), affirmed on DFAT internal review, that he may be directly or indirectly associated with the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction. The Tribunal declined to go behind the DFAT determination or grant a further adjournment, and affirmed the refusal; the second applicant could not meet family unit criteria.
