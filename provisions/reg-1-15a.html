October 2024 (1)

Devi (Migration)

7 Oct 2024Administrative Review Tribunal[2024] AATA 4008Tribunal reviewVisa refusal reviewMigration
Before: Alison Murphy
Ms Devi sought review of a refusal to grant an Other Family (Residence) (Class BU) visa, specifically a Subclass 836 (Carer) visa. Her Carer Visa Assessment Certificate recorded an impairment rating of 10, below the required rating of 30. The Tribunal affirmed the refusal.
Key holding

Under reg 1.15AA(1)(c), a carer visa applicant must provide a certificate specifying an impairment rating equal to or exceeding the Minister-specified threshold of 30.

Regulation 1.15AA(1)(c) provides that the impairment rating specified in the certificate must be equal to, or exceed, the impairment rating specified by the Minister in a legislative instrument. The relevant instrument for these purposes is IMMI 07/012 and it specifies that the impairment rating for the purposes of reg 1.15AA(1)(c) is 30.

April 2021 (1)

Pham v Minister for Immigration, Citizenship Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs

13 Apr 2021FCCA[2021] FCCA 718Migration: court challengeMigration
The applicant sought judicial review of a Tribunal decision affirming refusal of a Partner (Residence) visa on the basis that the Tribunal was not satisfied the relationship was genuine. The applicant argued the Tribunal failed to make required findings under reg 1.15A and failed to consider statutory declarations. The Court found the Tribunal's reasons reflected genuine intellectual engagement with each mandatory criterion and that no jurisdictional error was made out, dismissing the application.

15 Mar 2021FCCA[2021] FCCA 480Migration: court challengeMigration
Mr Arhbal sought judicial review of a Tribunal decision affirming the refusal of his subclass 820 partner visa.
