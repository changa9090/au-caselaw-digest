Application by Communications, Electrical, Electronic, Energy, Information, Postal, Plumbing and Allied Services Union of Australia (128V)

9 Oct 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 3881Employment & workplace
The CEPU applied under s.437 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) for a protected action ballot order covering ACT employees under the ACT Public Sector Infrastructure Services Enterprise Agreement 2023-2026. The employer objected to the draft order, ballot agent eligibility and the clarity of certain ballot questions. The Commission granted the application, allowed amendments, and issued a protected action ballot order.
Key holding

Where proposed ballot questions describe the nature of proposed industrial action sufficiently clearly for employees to respond, the Commission must include them in a protected action ballot order required under s 443(1).

If the questions describe the nature of proposed industrial action in a sufficiently clear way such that employees are capable of responding to them, then there is no basis for the Commission not to include them in a protected action ballot order that it is required to make under s 443(1).
