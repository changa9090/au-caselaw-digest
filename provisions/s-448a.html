The Fair Work Commission determined on the papers a joint application by AIMPE and AMOU for a protected action ballot order concerning employees of Inco Ships Pty Ltd. National Tertiary Education Industry Union v The University of Western Australia [2026] FWC 794 (10 March 2026)

10 Mar 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 794Employment & workplace
The NTEU applied under s.437 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) for a protected action ballot order covering certain University of Western Australia employees. UWA in effect did not object, and the Commission determined the matter on the papers. The Commission granted the application, approved TrueVote to conduct the ballot, set voting to close on 26 March 2026, and referred the matter to a s.448A compulsory conciliation conference.
Key holding

The Commission must make a protected action ballot order once satisfied of the s.443(1) prerequisites, including notification time and genuine bargaining.

On the basis of the material before me, including the declaration of Julia Moore, Industrial Officer, setting out the steps taken by the NTEU in bargaining with the Employer and that it has been, and is, genuinely trying to reach agreement with UWA, I am satisfied that there is a notification time in relation to the proposed agreement and that all of the requirements in s.443(1) of the Act have been met.
