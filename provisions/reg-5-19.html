Hurmat-Ul-Ain v Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs (No 2)

28 Jun 2024Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia[2024] FedCFamC2G 580Migration: court challengeTribunal reviewVisa nominationMigration
The applicant sought review of a Registrar's orders dismissing her judicial review application concerning the Tribunal's affirmation of a delegate's refusal to grant her a Subclass 187 Regional Employer Nomination visa. The Tribunal assumed the applicant could support her application with a nomination from a person other than the deregistered sponsor HVMP, which was legally incorrect under Singh's construction of reg 5.19, but the error was not material because the criterion in cl 187.223(3) could not be satisfied regardless.

April 2024 (1)

Coolah Supermarkets Pty Ltd v Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs

12 Apr 2024Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia[2024] FedCFamC2G 327Migration: court challengeMigration
Coolah Supermarkets and Mr Singh sought judicial review of a Tribunal decision refusing an employer nomination and a Regional Employer Nomination (Permanent) (Class RN) visa. The Court found the Tribunal incorrectly conducted a qualitative assessment of the need for an employee under reg 5.19(4)(a)(ii) at the first stage, but held reg 5.19 does not create jurisdictional facts and any error was immaterial because the genuine need requirement under reg 5.19(4)(h)(ii)(B) would inevitably have failed. Both applications were dismissed.

May 2021 (1)

Sri Guru Gobind Singh Transport Pty Ltd v Minister For Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs

31 May 2021FCCA[2021] FCCA 1191Migration: court challengeMigration
The Federal Circuit Court reviewed two Administrative Appeals Tribunal decisions: one cancelling the applicant company's approval as a standard business sponsor under s 140L, and one refusing a nomination under reg 5.19. The applicant alleged jurisdictional errors including misconstruction of reg 1.13A 'adverse information', failure to investigate, and illogical or irrational reasoning. The Court found no jurisdictional error disclosed and dismissed both applications.

Harinsco Pty Ltd v Minister for Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs

18 Mar 2021FCCA[2021] FCCA 528Migration: court challengeMigration
Harinsco Pty Ltd sought judicial review of a Tribunal decision refusing its Direct Entry Employer Nomination under reg 5.19 of the Migration Regulations. The nomination had been made for a position at Goolwa, but the applicant later attempted to change the location to McLaren Vale, and the Tribunal found the nominated position no longer existed. The Federal Circuit Court found no jurisdictional error, no illogicality or unreasonableness, and dismissed the application.
