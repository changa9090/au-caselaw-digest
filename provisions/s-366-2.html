Ms Vikki Turner v Ithaca Crystals Pty Ltd

21 Oct 2026Fair Work Commission[2026] FWC 4080Employment & workplace
Ms Turner lodged an unfair dismissal application under s 394 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth). Her former employer asserted she was not protected because it was a small business and she had not completed the minimum employment period. Ms Turner sought to amend to a general protections claim under s 586 (with an alternative extension of time under s 366(2)); the Commission held the application was in substance a general protections claim filed on the wrong form and allowed the amendment.
Key holding

Although s 586 cannot be used merely to substitute a general protections cause of action for an unfair dismissal claim so as to avoid ss 725/729, the Commission may waive the irregularity and permit amendment where the initiating application was in substance a general protections claim made on the wrong approved form.

Though there is no power to amend an application to substitute a general protections cause of action for an unfair dismissal one, that does not mean that the form in which the application is made is determinative. If a party simply uses the wrong form to make one type of claim or the other, that is an irregularity that can be corrected or waived pursuant to s 586 of the Fair Work Act: Hambridge v Spotless Facilities Services Pty Ltd
